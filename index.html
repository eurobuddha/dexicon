How this works
For ERC20 USDT holders. Both AtomiX markets are quoted side by side — USDT → MxUSD and USDT → MINIMA — tap the route you want. Your USDT locks on Ethereum first; the maker locks the coin; this node claims it automatically into its wallet, ready to trade.
One Ethereum transaction per part (gas paid from the deposit address above; a first-ever use also needs a one-time USDT approval). If no maker completes within 2 h your USDT is refunded to the deposit address. Holding MINIMA or MxUSD already? Send it to the receive address on ASSETS — no AtomiX swap needed.

How this works
Both AtomiX markets quoted side by side: MxUSD → USDT and MINIMA → USDT, each for the amount typed in its own coin — tap the route you want. Your coin locks on Minima; the maker locks USDT on Ethereum; this node withdraws it, then forwards it by one ERC20 transfer to the address you name.
Two Ethereum transactions are paid from the deposit address above: the withdraw of the maker’s USDT and the forward to your destination — both gas fees are shown in the quote. If no maker completes within ~2 h your MxUSD is refunded to this node’s wallet.

Market maker — FAST ladder

    
Your FAST ladder for MINIMA / MxUSD (price = MxUSD per MINIMA). ASKS — where YOU SELL MINIMA (higher). BIDS — where YOU BUY (lower). Each rung is one fast order: instant to place, reprice and cancel; your node co-signs each fill. Leave rows blank to skip them. A filled rung is topped back up to its size.

    
    
    
    

    
While pegged, the ladder regenerates around the MEXC MINIMA/USDT mid (± step %, your size per rung) and each rung is repriced the moment the market moves ≥ your threshold — a fast order edit, no block. If the feed goes stale the spread widens (up to ×10); with no price at all the ladder is withdrawn, then restored when it recovers. MEXC's MINIMA market is THIN — keep step % above its typical spread and sizes small; you auto-trade at these prices.
