Create a pool and earn 0.5%

Put MINIMA and MxUSD into your own pool. Every swap through it pays you 0.5%. The coins stay at your address, under your key.

You need: MINIMA and MxUSD in equal valueCommitment: keep Dexicon running regularly; keep the backup safeEarns: 0.5% of every swap through your pool

  1. Open My LP

    In ② Pools › My LP, tap Create a pool.

  2. Read the two obligations

    A pool’s reserve coins are re-created about every 900 blocks, so the Dexicon client must run regularly. You must also keep the pool file and your wallet seed safe.

    Turn off battery optimisation for Dexicon. It is the most common reason a pool goes dark.

  3. Choose the size

    Enter the MxUSD side. The MINIMA side is worked out at the current market price.

  4. Confirm the pool

    The summary shows both sides and the opening price. Tap Create.

  5. It is created on chain

    Your node locks both coins under the pool contract at your own address.

  6. Save the backup

    Save the backup file somewhere off the phone. It holds the pool recipe and an encrypted node backup.

  7. Backup verified

    Dexicon reads the file back and confirms it can restore your pool.

  8. Your pool

    The pool card shows your liquidity, its value now, fees earned and its health. Add, Migrate or Close it whenever you choose.
