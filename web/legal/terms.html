Terms of use

These terms cover the dexicon.org website. They do not create any service, because there is no Dexicon service: the protocol runs on its users’ own nodes.

What this site is

dexicon.org is an information site about an open protocol, published by the Dexicon contributors. It describes software anyone can run, links to its source and shows a read-only view of public orders. It does not hold funds, take orders, match trades, route payments or act for anyone.

Nobody profits from running it

Dexicon charges no fees and nobody takes a cut. The only fee anywhere is 0.5% on swaps against a liquidity pool, and all of it goes to the users who funded that pool. The contributors and the site’s publisher earn nothing from anyone’s trades.
