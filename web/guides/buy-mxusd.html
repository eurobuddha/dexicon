Buy MxUSD with USDT

Swap USDT on Ethereum for MxUSD on Minima, directly with a maker. Both sides lock in time-locked contracts, so either both get paid or both get their coins back.

You need: USDT and a little ETH for gas in your node’s Ethereum wallet (Assets › Fund / QR)Time: 2–10 minutesFee: none; Ethereum gas only

  1. Open the MxUSD room

    Tap ① MxUSD, then Swap, and choose Buy MxUSD. The best price and the number of makers online are shown under the form.

  2. Enter how much USDT to send

    Type the amount. You see exactly what you will receive, how many parts the swap splits into and the worst price you accept (the slippage).

  3. Review and start

    The review lists every part, the total and the deadlines. Tap Start swap.

    Your USDT is locked on Ethereum, not sent. If the maker never locks their side, it comes back to you by itself after the deadline.

  4. Watch the checklist

    Each step ticks off: node connected, USDT locked, the maker locks their MxUSD, you claim.

  5. Follow it in Activity

    The swap stays live in Activity with every step timed. You can leave Dexicon; its background service carries on.

  6. The maker locks, you claim

    Once the maker’s MxUSD is locked on Minima, your node claims it with the secret. Claiming reveals the secret, which lets the maker collect your USDT.

  7. Done

    The row turns Done. The swap is final and the MxUSD is yours.

  8. MxUSD lands in Assets

    It shows as unconfirmed for a few blocks, then becomes spendable like any coin in your wallet.
