Your coins: split and consolidate

Each order and each trade needs its own coin. Split a large coin to trade more at once; consolidate many small ones before a big on-chain trade.

Where: Assets, in any roomTime: one blockFee: none

    Assets shows what is available to trade, what is unconfirmed and how many coins you hold.

  2. Split ×10

    Sends your balance to yourself as 10 equal coins, so several orders and trades can run at once.

  3. Submitted

    It is final after the next block. The coins stay in your wallet the whole time.
