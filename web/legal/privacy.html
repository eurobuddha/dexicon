Privacy

This site collects no accounts, sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.

What your browser contacts

  • dexicon.org. The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser) for security and troubleshooting, kept for 14 days.
  • Network nodes, for the live book. To show live orders, your browser reads public orders straight from nodes of the Parlons/Maxima network, starting with eurobuddha.com, store.eurobuddha.com and minimammr.com. Those nodes are run by independent network participants. Like any website, they see your IP address when your browser connects.
  • Links you click, such as GitHub or app downloads, take you to other sites with their own policies.

Fonts and files

Fonts and images are served from dexicon.org itself. No third-party fonts, scripts or embeds are loaded.

The software

The Dexicon clients run on your own device and node. Trades you make are public on the Minima and Ethereum blockchains, as all blockchain transactions are.
